What to Expect

These retreats are designed to provide space for participants to do The Work in a meditative way. The structure is deliberately simple:

Partner Inquiry

Participants take turns being facilitator and client, working on stressful thoughts from their own lives.

Noble Silence

Outside of inquiry sessions, we maintain silence, allowing the work to deepen.

Sitting Meditation

Regular periods of sitting meditation support the inquiry process.

Walking Meditation

Moving meditation helps integrate insights into the body.

Typical Schedule

Thursday Evening: Registration, opening session, harvesting beliefs, introduction to The Work.

Friday and Saturday: Full days of meditation and partner inquiry, with meals taken in silence.

Sunday: Morning meditation and inquiry, closing circle, sharing of experiences.

Open-Ended Inquiry

The approach we take has three invitations:

  1. Give yourself to silence and stillness — gently, patiently wait for deeper answers to surface.
  2. Simply report — share the answers you hear inside yourself without censoring or trying to figure out what the answers are "supposed" to be.
  3. Yield to time — allow inquiry to take as long as it may, respecting the natural rhythms of your own process.
